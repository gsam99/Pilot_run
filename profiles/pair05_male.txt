I'm 34, and I'm an accountant. I own a small practice - there are three of us, and we handle mostly restaurants and contractors, which means I've spent a decade watching people start businesses and noticing which ones survive. Latino, five foot seven, average build. Catholic, and reasonably serious about it, although I don't press it on anybody.

I'm cautious, and I recognize that isn't the most thrilling admission on a page like this. I would generally rather move slowly and be certain than move quickly and hope. I have not missed a filing deadline in eleven years, which tells you most of what you need to know about me. Difficult to provoke, and apparently easy to be around.

The garage is full of furniture I have built, and the earliest pieces are still in there where nobody has to look at them. I cook most evenings. I grow tomatoes and peppers in a plot considerably smaller than my ambitions for it, and I visit other people's restaurants constantly and take notes.

What I actually want, eventually, is a small farm with a restaurant on it. I have run the numbers more often than is healthy, so I know approximately what it would cost and how long it would take, and I would want to do it carefully. Come and find me if you make something with your hands.
