I'm 31. I studied computer science, then spent seven years building infrastructure at a couple of companies you'd probably recognize, and about a year ago I stopped. I'm not working at the moment and I haven't given myself a deadline, which appears to worry other people considerably more than it worries me. White, five foot nine, athletic.

By design, I'm not particularly organized. I'm slow answering messages and my apartment reflects a general indifference to surfaces. I'm difficult to upset, although I can't take much credit for it, since it appears to be temperament rather than any actual effort. And I enjoy arguing about ideas in a friendly sort of way, usually at unreasonable hours.

I snowboard often and ski when the snow isn't right for boarding. I've been sailing since childhood and I still crew on a friend's boat most summers. Lately I've been reading a great deal of Buddhism and Hindu philosophy, and I enjoy eating Indian vegetarian food. Actually, I've been on a vegan diet for several months now.

Argue with me about something and I will be happy, because intelligence probably matters to me more than anything else. For whatever it is worth about the longer term, I do want a family eventually, and to be making vegan dinners for it. I am simply not in any particular hurry about arriving there.
