I'm 31. I grow organic vegetables on two leased acres, and I make glass - bowls mostly, and some lamps - and I sell both at farmers markets and craft fairs. I did two years of college and then left to apprentice with a glassblower. White, five foot seven, and an atheist, although not a noisy one.

I'm easy to be around and I don't plan very much. I never know what I earned in a month until my accountant tells me, and I've stopped pretending that will change. But in eleven years I have missed exactly two markets, and I was ill on both occasions. I am careless about money and completely reliable about people.

The glass takes most of my winter and the vegetables take the remainder of the year. I swim in the river whenever the temperature permits. I cook whatever didn't sell, which means I eat beautifully in August and peculiarly in February. I like live music, generally in small venues.

A farm with a farm-to-table restaurant. That is the whole ambition, and I don't have a plan for it. I am honestly not certain I need one, since the last four things that worked for me I started before I was ready. Come to a market with me at six in the morning and we will see.
