I'm 31 and I work as a civil engineer for the county, mostly on bridges and culverts. I have a college degree from the state university. Five foot eleven, average build, white, and agnostic, although I'm not especially serious about it. My parents live two hours away, in the town where I grew up.

People tell me I'm easy to be around and I think that's mostly accurate. I notice when somebody has had a difficult week and I generally say so, which a few people find intrusive. I have never once been late collecting anybody from an airport. In a group I would rather book the table than run the evening.

There's an old frame I've been rebuilding in the garage since spring. Otherwise it's forty or fifty miles most weekends, when the weather is reasonable. I cook a proper meal most evenings and it is my favorite part of the day, usually with records on rather than a playlist.

Message me if you ride, or if you want to learn. Somebody funny would help. One thing to know about my weeks is that they look much the same as each other, and I plan my rides two weeks out. I am not a spontaneous person, and I wouldn't decide on a Friday evening to drive to the coast.
