I'm 28 and I teach art at an elementary school. I have a fine arts degree and a teaching certificate. Five foot four, curvy, white, and Christian. My faith is not a background detail; it determines how I make decisions. I live with my younger brother, who has cerebral palsy, and I am his primary caregiver.

I am patient, which the classroom requires and my brother requires considerably more of. I don't rattle. There are people who would be in genuine difficulty if I started letting things slide, so I don't let things slide. I am steadier than I am expressive, and I know that occasionally reads as coldness to somebody expecting more.

I direct the children's choir at church, and I take my Sunday School class seriously enough that I prepare it the way I prepare a school week. I sing hymns and contemporary worship music. I bake something for the class every Sunday. Most evenings I am at home with my brother.

Something you need to know before anything else: I cannot travel. My brother requires daily care and I am the person who provides it, and that situation will not change. Beyond that, sing with me rather than at me. And be kind to my brother, not only to me.
